## 🌍 What is Tourism Paradigm Engineering (TPE)?

For decades, the global tourism industry has operated on a flawed ontology: treating the traveler as a passive consumer of services and the destination as a passive supply of attractions. This approach has led to systemic issues—overtourism, host community burnout, and a failure to deliver genuine restorative benefits to travelers.

**Tourism Paradigm Engineering (TPE)** is a new, sovereign scientific discipline that fundamentally redesigns the rules of the game. It shifts the focus from *extractive transaction volume* to the *regulation of coupled human-environment systems*. 

At its core, TPE is built upon three foundational pillars:
1.  **Tourist Biophysics:** The traveler is modeled not as a customer, but as an open, far-from-equilibrium thermodynamic system that exports accumulated stress (entropy) and requires a structured environment for genuine physiological and neurological restoration (measurable via HRV, EEG, and EDA).
2.  **Tourism Cybernetics:** The destination is modeled as a living, regulated cybernetic organism. "Carrying capacity" is no longer a fixed number of beds, but a dynamic, time-varying variable regulated in real-time through closed-loop feedback systems.
3.  **Host as a Finite Resource:** The host community is treated as a "negative-entropy anchorage"—a finite, depletable stock that must be protected, calibrated, and maintained to prevent burnout and ensure sustainable reciprocity.

### 🎯 The Goal
The mission of TPE is to establish this engineering framework as the primary paradigm for global tourism by 2030. We aim to provide researchers, policymakers, and destination managers with **falsifiable, measurable, and ethical protocols** to engineer restorative journeys and anti-fragile destinations.
